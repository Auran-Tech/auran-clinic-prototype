# Product Decision Log
## D-001 — Platform Foundation is V1
**Status:** Approved.  
AURAN Platform Admin, clinic provisioning, creation of the initial protected clinic administrator/Super User, clinic suspension/reactivation and their security/session behaviors are official AURAN Clinic V1 foundation capabilities. This supersedes legacy specifications that listed Platform Administration as deferred.

## D-002 — Appointments and commercial billing remain deferred
Follow-up remains a recommendation workflow and must not silently become appointment scheduling. Subscription/plan/billing functionality is outside V1 until separately approved.
